15 November 2025 - 25 January 2026 ›
Consultation Open

FSC have developed two draft revised Standards which reflect feedback previously collected through the conceptual phase consultation and working group meetings.

These are:

  • FSC-STD-40-004; and
  • FSC-STD-20-011.

Public consultation on the first drafts of both standards was open from 15 November 2025 to 25 January 2026, in order to seek feedback from interested stakeholders.

The draft standards are available for download below.

20 - 22 May 2025 ›
Kick-off meeting

The Working Group kick-off meeting for the Chain of Custody Standard revision was held in Bonn, Germany on 20 - 22 May 2025.

11 December 2024 - 31 January 2025 ›
Open Call for Working Group Members Application

FSC opened the call for applications to join a working group to revise chain of custody standards.

Applicants could apply from 11 December to 31 January 2025 and identify their preferred workstreams.
